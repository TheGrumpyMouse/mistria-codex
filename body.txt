{{About|[[Cosmetics]] that alter the physical body of [[The Player]] like their [[Cosmetics_(Body)#Hairstyles|hairstyles]] and [[Cosmetics_(Body)#Facial_Hair|facial hair]]|[[Cosmetics_(Clothes)#Clothing|clothes]] and [[Cosmetics_(Clothes)#Shoes|shoes]]|Cosmetics (Clothes)|smaller cosmetics that alter [[The Player|The Player's]] physical appearance, like [[Accessories#Head Wear|head wear]], [[Accessories#Earrings|earrings]], [[Accessories#Glasses|glasses]], and [[Accessories#Back Accessories|capes]]|Accessories|the main page|Cosmetics}}

Below are [[Cosmetics]] that alter [[The Player|The Player's]] character's physical appearance, including hair, facial hair, and eyes.
==Acquisition==
While the bulk of the available body modifications are available from {{sourceIcon|Character creation}}, new [[#Hairstyles|Hairstyles]] can be purchased from {{SourceIcon|Saturday Short|Vera}}.
